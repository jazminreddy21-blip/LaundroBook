About LaundroBook

Making Laundry
Simple for Everyone

LaundroBook is a modern online laundry booking platform that allows customers to reserve washing machines, schedule laundry deliveries and monitor the progress of their laundry from any device. Our goal is to make laundry services more convenient, reliable and accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

Why We Created LaundroBook

Traditional laundromats often involve long waits, unavailable machines and uncertainty. LaundroBook was designed to eliminate these problems by providing a simple online booking and tracking experience.

Easy Booking

Reserve a washing machine before arriving and avoid waiting in long queues. We understand how tedious waiting in line can be, which is why we allow customers to book remotely and save valuable time.

Delivery Tracking

Monitor your laundry delivery in real time and know exactly when it will arrive. Instead of leaving home to fetch your laundry, we will deliver it directly to your doorstep.

Progress Tracking

Receive updates when your laundry is cleaned and ready for collection. Monitor the progress of your laundry and schedule collections that fit your daily routine.
